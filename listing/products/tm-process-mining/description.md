Tm Process Mining shows how work really moves through your business, inside your Qlik Cloud apps. Nine visuals cover the routes Cases take, the Variants behind them, where work loops back, and how two groups of Cases differ. Click a route, a step or a Case and the rest of the sheet follows. Analysts use it to find delays and rework before they ask for a new report. The visuals read your event data and never change it. They work best when each Case has a start, an end and timestamped Events.
